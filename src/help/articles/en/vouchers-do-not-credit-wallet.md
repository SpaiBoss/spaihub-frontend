## Terms

A **voucher** is a prepaid code **SPAI-XXXX-XXXX** plus a **WiFi PIN**. You generate it under **Vouchers**, print it, sell it for cash (or credit) at the counter.

**Wallet** **Available Balance** is MoMo money SpaiHub can send you via **Withdraw**. Hint on the wallet: MoMo sales credit your wallet after the platform fee. **Voucher stock you sold offline does not add wallet balance.**

**Redeem** is the guest button **Redeem voucher**. That consumes the code and grants Wi‑Fi. It is not a Campay payment and not a wallet top-up.

**Reserved for contributors** is unrelated voucher cash. It is OPEN contributor accruals held on the owner wallet. Contributors themselves see one balance.

## Why it matters

Incident from a Makepe kiosk: owner printed 50 tickets, sold 40 in a weekend, opened **Wallet**, saw the same Available as Friday, and thought SpaiHub “ate the voucher money.” Staff almost refunded a MoMo buyer to “make the wallet move.” The wallet never owed them those 40 tickets. The cash is in the till. That is the product.

Wrong diagnosis: “Sync unused to router will credit wallet when they come online.” Sync only queues GRANT for unused codes onto the Hex. No XAF.

Wrong diagnosis: “If I **Revoke**, the wallet should go down.” Revoke stops a code. It does not unwind cash you already held.

If you need digital settlement, sell **Pay with MoMo**. If you need float in the till, sell vouchers. Mixing the two in your head creates fake disputes.

A second Douala pattern: contributor standing at the shop sees **Wallet** on their own app, hears “we sold fifty tickets,” and expects their **Contributor balance** to jump. Contributor payouts are uplink accruals, not ticket paper. Do not drag them into a voucher-wallet argument.

## What you see

**Vouchers**: **Create vouchers**, **Print PDF**, **Sync unused to router**, statuses **Unused**, **Redeemed**, **Expired**, **Revoked**.

**Wallet**: **Available Balance**, **Available to withdraw**, **Wallet total**, **Reserved for contributors** (if any), **Withdraw**, **Withdrawal History**. There is no voucher ledger line “ticket #12 +500 XAF.”

**Transactions** lists portal payments. Voucher redemptions are not MoMo rows that raise Available.

Dashboard **Payment mix** can show voucher vs MoMo *business* volume for your eyes. That still is not cash SpaiHub is holding for **Withdraw**.

## What to do

1. Put voucher cash in your till book, not in **Wallet**.
2. After **Create vouchers**, **Print PDF** from **Unused**. Do not expect Available to tick up.
3. Use **Withdraw** only against MoMo **Available to withdraw**.
4. If a buyer paid MoMo *and* you also sold them a voucher for the same hour, that is your shop error — SpaiHub will not merge those into one wallet story.
5. Explain at the counter: ticket = cash to the shop; phone Pay = wallet later, minus fee.
6. If **Available Balance** did not move after a busy cash night, check **Transactions** for MoMo only — then look in the till book, not at **Withdraw**.

## What not to say

- Do not tell a guest “your voucher is in my SpaiHub wallet.” It is not.
- Do not promise remaining fair-use GB on a time voucher. Same hidden-cap rules.
- Do not say a queued withdrawal failed because today’s voucher sales “did not arrive.”
- Do not invent an admin screen to “push voucher XAF.” Not in owner Help.
