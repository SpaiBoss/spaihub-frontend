## Terms

A **Location** is one hotspot **site** you sell from: a shop in Akwa, a courtyard in the cité, a corridor kiosk. In the app it is a row under **Locations** (short **Sites**).

It **includes**:

- One or more **routers** (physical Hex or CHR rows)
- **Packages** (time or data plans in XAF)
- **Sessions** (who is online)
- **Access policy** (fallback device count)

It is **not**:

- A **cheap extender** or travel router (Pixlink, Tenda, no-name — hardware on a wall)
- A **subscriber** / buyer / phone
- A voucher code
- Your owner **email** account
- A contributor **link** (physical uplink SpaiHub attached for a contributor)

**Address** is a label for staff. It is not a GPS product.

## Why it matters

People say “location” meaning “the Wi‑Fi,” “the extender,” or “the guy who paid.” If staff mix those, they **Kick** the wrong story, **Suspend** the whole shop when they meant one voucher, or tell a contributor that their uplink *is* the location.

Owners create locations **without** a live Hex. That is normal: add the site, add packages, **Preview portal**, then paste scripts. Contributors may see a location **name** on **Links** because their uplink sits at that shop — they still do not get owner **Add Location**.

## What you see

**Locations** → **Add Location** → **Location name**, **Address** → **Create**. Toast **Location created**.

Expand a row: tabs **Routers**, **Packages**, **Sessions**, **Access policy**. Counts like **N/M routers online**, **N active sessions**.

**Suspend** / **Activate**: suspending stops new portal sales at that site without deleting history. **Edit location** → **Save changes**.

Empty: **No locations yet** — add your first hotspot location to start deploying routers and selling packages.

Contributor **Home** / **Links** may list **Location** as a column on an uplink. That is a name, not a full owner dashboard.

## What to do

1. Name locations like shops (“Akwa corridor”), not like routers (“Hex-3”) or extenders (“repeater salon”).
2. Put packages on the location guests actually join. A second courtyard with its own SSID is usually a second location (or at least a second router row you understand).
3. Do not **Suspend** because one cheap extender is NAT-sharing. Suspend is the whole site’s new sales.
4. Contributors: if a link shows a location, questions about packages and Kick go to the shop owner, not to your **Wallet**.

## What not to say

- Do not call a cheap extender “a SpaiHub location.”
- Do not tell a guest “your phone is a location.”
- Do not quote remaining fair-use GB when explaining which site they are on.
- Do not promise a contributor they can create locations in their app.
