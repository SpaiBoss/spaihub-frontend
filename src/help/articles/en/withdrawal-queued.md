## Terms

**Withdraw** is the wallet button that opens the payout form. Owners also see **Withdraw to MoMo** and **Submit Withdrawal**. Contributors see **Submit withdrawal**.

A **queued** withdrawal means SpaiHub accepted the request and will complete the MoMo transfer shortly when automatic send cannot finish immediately. It is **not** **Withdrawal failed**.

Owner toasts you may see:

- **Withdrawal sent to your MoMo** — automatic send worked.
- **Withdrawal is queued. An admin will complete the MoMo transfer shortly.**
- Short: **Withdrawal queued for processing**

Contributor toast: **Withdrawal queued** (versus **Withdrawal sent to your MoMo** or **Withdrawal failed**).

**Available to withdraw** (owner) or **Contributor balance** (contributor) must cover the amount. Queued does not mean the money vanished.

## Why it matters

Friday evening, MTN is slow. You tap **Submit Withdrawal** once, see queued, panic, tap again, and risk two requests. The first toast already said success-shaped: queued for processing. Treating queued as failure is how duplicate payouts start.

Incident: contributor in Douala screenshotted **Withdrawal queued**, emailed the shop owner “SpaiHub refused me.” The owner then tried to “fix” it by withdrawing the same story from the owner wallet. Two products. The contributor request was already accepted.

Wrong diagnosis: “queued means I used voucher money.” Vouchers never credited the wallet. Wrong diagnosis: “queued means fair use blocked the SIM.” Unrelated.

You may see the word admin on the owner toast. That is the on-screen sentence. This article does not describe an admin console.

Network dips in Douala do this often at 18h. The request is in **Withdrawal History**. Your MoMo SMS may arrive minutes later. Standing in the shop refreshing **Wallet** every five seconds does not speed Campay. Retrying does. Wait for sent, queued, or an honest **Withdrawal failed**.

## What you see

Owner **Wallet** → **Withdraw** → amount in XAF, Cameroon number, **MTN MoMo** or **Orange Money** → **Submit Withdrawal**. Sending copy: **Sending withdrawal to your MoMo — please wait…**

Contributor **Wallet** → **Withdraw** → **Amount (XAF)**, **Phone Number** → **Submit withdrawal**.

**Withdrawal History** (owner) or **Withdrawal history** (contributor) lists requests. Queued/pending rows are not empty failures. **Withdrawal failed** is the actual failure toast.

Minimum owner amount copy: **Minimum 100 XAF**. **Max … XAF available** respects contributor reserve on owner wallets.

## What to do

Tonight:

1. Submit **once**. Wait for sent or queued.
2. If queued, stop. Check history rather than retrying the same amount.
3. Confirm the MoMo number in **Settings** (contributor **MoMo phone**, owner form **Phone Number**).
4. If you see **Withdrawal failed**, then you may retry — still once.
5. Do not collect a “manual cash compensation” from the shop till because a queued toast scared you, then also receive the MoMo later.

## What not to say

- Do not tell a partner “queued means rejected.”
- Do not promise a guest anything about your withdrawal. Guests are on the portal, not Wallet.
- Do not mention remaining fair-use GB in a payout argument.
- Do not describe how an internal operator retries Campay. If the toast mentioned an admin, wait for the MoMo SMS.
